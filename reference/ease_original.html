    Built around the first ten minutes

    Every feature exists to get a clear, usable report to a human decision-maker quickly, and to keep that person in control.

    🚨

    Report in seconds

    One red button, plain-language urgency, short text or voice. GPS is optional with a manual address fallback. Nothing blocks the report.

    🧭

    Shortest-path routing

    Dijkstra’s algorithm over a road graph of Dehradun finds the nearest suitable facility. A routing demo, not live traffic.

    ⚑

    Fraud signals, human decisions

    Duplicate and abuse signals are explained and scored apart from severity. A reviewer decides. A flag never delays dispatch.

    📍

    Clear report status

    Citizens see what is confirmed and what is simulated, with a timeline from submitted to resolved.

    🗂

    Dispatch workspace

    Priority queue, map, eligibility-checked assignment and reassignment with reasons.

    🧾

    Audit trail

    Every consequential action records who, when, why and the previous value.

    The journey of a report

    From a frightened caller to a tracked response.

    Report

    A citizen describes the situation. Location is attached if available, or typed.

    Triage

    The command centre sees severity, location and review signals side by side.

    Dispatch

    An operator assigns an available, suitable unit, with distance from the road graph.

    Track

    The citizen follows status and the unit’s approach (simulated in this demo).

    Two portals, one engine

    Sign in to the one that fits your role. Citizens can also report without an account.

    EASE Citizen

    For the public. Mobile-first and laptop-friendly.

    • Report an emergency, with or without an account
    • Track your reports and live status
    • Find nearby hospitals, fire and police (demo data)
    • Manage language, notifications and privacy

    EASE Command Centre

    For dispatchers, supervisors and administrators.

    • Live queue, map and incident detail
    • Dispatch and responder management
    • Risk review, analytics and audit log
    • Role-based access for staff accounts

    Honest by design

    EASE is a B.Tech final-year project. Here is exactly what is real and what is simulated.

    Working in this demo

    • Full citizen reporting flow, duplicate checks and offline queue
    • Command Centre queue, dispatch, review and audit
    • Dijkstra routing on a real OpenStreetMap road snapshot
    • Account sign-in with hashed passwords (demo store)

    Simulated or not yet connected

    • Responder GPS, live traffic and real dispatch are simulated and labelled
    • OTP codes are shown on screen. No SMS is sent
    • Accounts live in this browser. Production needs a server (Supabase Auth, RLS)
    • No real emergency service is contacted. Call 112 in danger
